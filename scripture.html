The Scripture Index

Every passage, and what it anchors — set beside the text itself, so that you can check the claim without leaving the page.

What this is

The Scriptural Appendix tells you which books this road draws on. This goes the other way: it works through Scripture passage by passage and records what each one anchors — which stage, which chapter, which practice, and which of the three pillars it serves. Where a passage anchors nothing, it is left alone rather than stretched to fit.

The text is the World English Bible, which is in the public domain, so the passage can sit here in full rather than as a reference you have to go and look up. The whole index is CC0, like everything else here.

How to read an entry

The tags are claims, and claims can be wrong.

Each entry carries a confidence marking. A passage marked medium is one where the connection seemed real but not certain — treat it as a question rather than a finding. Entries marked author placed were assigned by the author directly.

If a connection here does not survive your own reading of the passage, the passage wins. That is the same instruction the Scriptural Appendix gives, and it is meant just as plainly here.
