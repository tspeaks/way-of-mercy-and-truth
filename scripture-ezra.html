Ezra 3:10-13

The old men who had seen the first house… wept with a loud voice; and many shouted aloud for joy: so that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping

10When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of Yahweh, they set the priests in their clothing with trumpets, and the Levites the sons of Asaph with cymbals, to praise Yahweh, after the order of David king of Israel.

11They sang one to another in praising and giving thanks to Yahweh, “For he is good, for his loving kindness endures forever toward Israel.” All the people shouted with a great shout, when they praised Yahweh, because the foundation of the house of Yahweh was laid.

12But many of the priests and Levites and heads of fathers’ households, the old men who had seen the first house, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice; and many shouted aloud for joy:

13so that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping of the people; for the people shouted with a loud shout, and the noise was heard afar off.

The foundation of the second temple is laid, and two sounds rise together. 'All the people shouted with a great shout… But many of the priests and Levites… the old men who had seen the first house, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice; and many shouted aloud for joy: so that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping.' Sacred Lament held beside praise rather than replacing it (medium). C25's small beginning, honestly felt by those who remember what was lost, and C13's community that makes room for both.

Ezra 7:10

Ezra had set his heart to seek the law of Yahweh, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and ordinances

10For Ezra had set his heart to seek the law of Yahweh, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and ordinances.

One sentence describing a teacher's formation in its proper order: 'For Ezra had set his heart to seek the law of Yahweh, and to do it, and to teach.' Study, then practice, then teaching — C05's apprenticeship, and C04's insistence that the doer comes before the teacher.

Ezra 8:21-23

I was ashamed to ask of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way, because we had spoken to the king, saying, 'The hand of our God is on all those who seek him'

21Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river Ahava, that we might humble ourselves before our God, to seek of him a straight way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.

22For I was ashamed to ask of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way, because we had spoken to the king, saying, “The hand of our God is on all those who seek him, for good; but his power and his wrath is against all those who forsake him.”

23So we fasted and begged our God for this: and he was entreated of us.

Before a dangerous journey with the temple treasure, Ezra proclaims a fast 'that we might humble ourselves before our God, to seek of him a straight way.' The reason is consistency: 'I was ashamed to ask of the king a band of soldiers… because we had spoken to the king, saying, "The hand of our God is on all those who seek him, for good."' C12's proof — a stated trust tested by whether one acts on it — and C22's dependence.

Ezra 9:5-9

My God, I am ashamed and blush to lift up my face to you… you, our God, have punished us less than our iniquities deserve… a little reviving in our bondage

5At the evening offering I arose up from my humiliation, even with my garment and my robe torn; and I fell on my knees, and spread out my hands to Yahweh my God;

6and I said, “My God, I am ashamed and blush to lift up my face to you, my God; for our iniquities have increased over our head, and our guiltiness has grown up to the heavens.

7Since the days of our fathers we have been exceeding guilty to this day; and for our iniquities we, our kings, and our priests, have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, to plunder, and to confusion of face, as it is this day.

8Now for a little moment grace has been shown from Yahweh our God, to leave us a remnant to escape, and to give us a nail in his holy place, that our God may lighten our eyes, and give us a little reviving in our bondage.

9For we are bondservants; yet our God has not forsaken us in our bondage, but has extended loving kindness to us in the sight of the kings of Persia, to give us a reviving, to set up the house of our God, and to repair its ruins, and to give us a wall in Judah and in Jerusalem.

'I fell on my knees, and spread out my hands to Yahweh my God; and I said, "My God, I am ashamed and blush to lift up my face to you, my God; for our iniquities have increased over our head."' Self-Accusation in the first person, from a leader who had not committed the offence in question. And inside the shame, gratitude for a small mercy: 'for a little moment grace has been shown… give us a little reviving in our bondage.' C04 and C19. The index anchors the posture of the prayer only; the policy that follows it (the dissolution of mixed marriages in chapter 10) is not anchored, as the coverage note records.
