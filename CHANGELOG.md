# Changelog

All notable changes to the trunk. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

The genealogy layer is living and not versioned with the trunk; its changes are not
tracked here.

## [1.0.1] — Genealogy layer and licensing change

### Added

- **`genealogy/`** — the development record, as a peer layer to the trunk rather than part
  of it. Ten sections, `WMT G00`–`WMT G09`, covering the philosophical prehistory, the
  ethical nucleus, Johannine interiorization, Syriac formation and the Upright/Perfect
  problem, cosmological context, the integration into Theory and Praxis, the Harrowing
  and Iapetid material, civilizational praxis, and the architecture with its cautions.
- **`ORGANIZATION.md`** — the boundary between the free layer and any commercial work.
- Genealogy indexed in `llms.txt` and `manifest.json`, with a `status` field so
  speculative material is machine-visible as such.

### Changed

- **License moved from CC BY-SA 4.0 to CC0 1.0.** The curriculum, genealogy, and commons
  materials are dedicated to the public domain: no permission, no attribution
  requirement, commercial use permitted, model training explicitly welcomed. The
  governance rule about naming your branch and keeping history visible survives as a
  stated norm rather than a license condition.

### Note

`WMT G07` (the Harrowing and the Iapetids) is marked `status: speculative`. It is recorded
because it is a real part of the project's development, and fenced because nothing in the
curriculum depends on it.

## [1.0.0] — First public release

First versioned release of the complete curriculum: twenty-nine chapters in seven
stages, three appendices, thirty practices.

### Changed from the pre-release manuscript

**The narrator's frame was corrected for internal consistency.** The earlier draft was
framed as letters received from an unnamed teacher over several decades. No such
correspondence existed; the material was assembled by the author directly from the
sources. Because the book asks its reader to check rather than trust, a fabricated
transmission story could not stand in it.

- The Preface now describes the actual search: sources found, ordered, and tested
  firsthand, without a mentor.
- References to the teacher's instruction across Chapters 3, 4, 9, 10, and 23 are
  reattributed to the author's own study or to the ancient writers by name.
- Claims of age and elapsed duration were removed or de-specified. The narrator no
  longer claims a span he has not lived.
- Added **A Note on the Pseudonym** to the back matter, disclosing that "A. Pilgrim" is
  a pseudonym and why.

The teaching itself is unchanged. What changed is that the book now meets its own
standard: what is checkable is checkable, and what is not is disclosed as frame.
