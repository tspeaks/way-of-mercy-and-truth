24Then Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah came near, and struck Micaiah on the cheek, and said, “Which way did the Spirit of Yahweh go from me to speak to you?”

25Micaiah said, “Behold, you will see on that day, when you go into an inner room to hide yourself.”

26The king of Israel said, “Take Micaiah, and carry him back to Amon the governor of the city, and to Joash the king’s son.

27Say, ‘Thus says the king, “Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I come in peace.”’”

28Micaiah said, “If you return at all in peace, Yahweh has not spoken by me.” He said, “Listen, all you people!”

Four hundred prophets tell two kings what they want to hear. Jehoshaphat asks whether there is another. 'There is yet one man… Micaiah the son of Imlah; but I hate him; for he does not prophesy good concerning me, but evil.' The messenger coaches him on the way: 'See now, the prophets declare good to the king with one mouth. Please let your word be like the word of one of them, and speak good.' Micaiah: 'As Yahweh lives, what Yahweh says to me, that I will speak.' C08's true prophet set against a unanimous court. He is struck in the face and sent to prison on 'bread of affliction,' and his last word is the test Deuteronomy 18:22 prescribes: 'If you return at all in peace, Yahweh has not spoken by me.'
