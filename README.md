# The Way of Mercy and Truth

*A Curriculum of Formation in Three Pillars and Seven Stages*

> Truth without mercy turns harsh. Mercy without truth turns sentimental.
> Humility keeps them both honest.

A twenty-nine chapter curriculum of Christian formation, drawn from the Christian East —
Aphrahat, the Book of Steps, the Macarian Homilies, Isaac of Nineveh, Cassian, Ephrem,
the Didache — and ordered not by chronology or theological importance, but according to
the order in which a human being actually changes.

Free to read, quote, translate, and redistribute.
**[Read it at wayofmercyandtruth.org](https://wayofmercyandtruth.org)** — or take the
[full plain text](dist/wmt-full.txt) in one file.

## Start here

**Do not read this quickly.** One chapter per week is the intended pace. Twenty-nine
weeks — rather more than half a year. That is not slow; it is the natural speed of the
thing.

| If you are… | Start with | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **New to this** | [Preface](trunk/preface.md) (~15 min) | [Chapter One](trunk/chapters/c01-the-ground-beneath-everything.md), then one chapter per week |
| **Wanting the method first** | [Praxis Appendix](trunk/appendices/praxis-appendix.md) — all 30 practices, rated by difficulty | Pick **two**. Hold them a year. Then add one more. |
| **Checking the sources** | [Scriptural Appendix](trunk/appendices/scriptural-appendix.md) | A Note on the Witnesses — where this departs from each source, and why |
| **Leading a group** | [Praxis Appendix](trunk/appendices/praxis-appendix.md) | The reflection questions in each chapter (not the discussion questions you'll be tempted to invent) |

**The single most important instruction in the book:** take up two practices, not
thirty. Hold them for a year. The person who attempts all of them will keep none of them
past Thursday.

## The shape of it

Seven stages, each answering one question:

| | Stage | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | [Foundations](trunk/stages/s1-foundations.md) | How should one live? |
| **2** | [Perception](trunk/stages/s2-perception.md) | How should one see? |
| **3** | [Abiding](trunk/stages/s3-abiding.md) | How does one remain? |
| **4** | [Community](trunk/stages/s4-community.md) | How should people live together? |
| **5** | [Healing](trunk/stages/s5-healing.md) | How is the heart healed? |
| **6** | [Transformation](trunk/stages/s6-transformation.md) | How does the heart change? |
| **7** | [Mature Mercy](trunk/stages/s7-mature-mercy.md) | What does the transformed person become? |

Three pillars run through all seven: **Truth, Humility, Mercy.**

And one formula the whole thing rests on:

> Grace provides the fire. Faith prepares the altar. Works are the fruit that preserves
> the walk.

You are not building a ladder. You are building an altar.

## What this is not

**This is not a church, and it is not a substitute for one.**

This book has no authority over you. It cannot absolve you, baptize you, feed you at any
table, marry or bury you, or stand between you and God. Belong to a real congregation.
Sit under teaching that is not this. Be accountable to someone who can see your face.

If what you find here conflicts with your tradition, your tradition is older and has
thought about these questions longer.

If this ever becomes a reason to stay home on Sunday with a notebook instead, it has
harmed you, and you should put it down.

## Check this work

> I would rather you checked me than trusted me. That is not modesty; it is the only
> arrangement in which a teacher can be safely useful.

Every scripture claim is mapped in the [Scriptural Appendix](trunk/appendices/scriptural-appendix.md).
Every departure from a source is disclosed in A Note on the Witnesses. If the teaching
and the text disagree, discard the teaching.

Found an error? [Open an issue](../../issues). Corrections are the mechanism working, not

## Where this came from

The [`genealogy/`](genealogy/) directory is the development record — the sources, the
order they arrived in, and the problem the seven stages were built to solve. It answers
*where did this come from?*, where the curriculum answers *what does this do?*

You do not need it to walk the Way. Read it if you want to know why the road is shaped
this way.
