James 1:2-4

Trials produce steadfastness

2Count it all joy, my brothers, when you fall into various temptations,

3knowing that the testing of your faith produces endurance.

4Let endurance have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing.

Endurance is described as a process with a finished state, not a mood. This is the same claim the Discipline of Gradualism makes: that formation has a shape and a duration, and that shortening it is not available.
