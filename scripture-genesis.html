Covering Weakness is the refusal to expose someone whose failure would shame them, absorbing the inconvenience oneself to protect their dignity. The builders' motive is stated in their own words: 'let's build ourselves a city, and a tower whose top reaches to the sky, and let's make ourselves a name, lest we be scattered.' Achievement aimed at reputation, and security sought in a monument. The Hidden Life is the practice against exactly this — the reach for the audience, the energy spent on being perceived — and the tower is its absence at the scale of a civilisation, with its cost: the work is stopped and the builders scattered. C18's detachment is the other half: the tower is built to hold on. Confidence is medium; the passage is about collective ambition, and the practice is personal.
