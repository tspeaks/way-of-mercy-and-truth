# Revelation — review package

Scripture: World English Bible (public domain). Index status: complete. Method version 2.3.

Every entry below shows the passage in full, then the claim made about it. Read the passage before the claim. The question is not whether the tags are internally consistent — it is whether they survive contact with the text.

**Note.** John's apocalypse: the letters to seven churches, the throne and the Lamb, the judgments, the fall of Babylon, and the new creation. Indexed selectively: the letters and the visions of worship and renewal are indexed; the judgment cycles are not. Not cited by the curriculum or the Scriptural Appendix.

**Prediction note.** Forecast: the letters (2-3) the formation core, with 2:4-5 for PR11 and 3:17 for PR28; 21:3-4 the C19 terminus. Outcome: as forecast. The book closes the canon by answering lament (21:4) and Exodus 33:20 (22:4).

**Coverage note.** Indexed selectively. Not indexed: 1:1-8 and 1:19-20; the letters to Pergamum and Thyatira (2:12-29), whose charges concern specific false teachings the index does not interpret; the seals, trumpets and bowls (6, 8-11, 15-16); 7:1-8; the woman, the dragon and the beasts (12:1-9, 12:13-14:20), and the number of the beast; the harlot (17); 18:1-10 and 18:18-24; the rider, the millennium and the last judgment (19:11-20:15); the measurements of the city (21:8-21); and the closing warnings (22:6-16, 22:18-21). The index does not interpret Revelation's symbols or adopt any scheme for its chronology.

## 1. Revelation 1:9-18 — I John, your brother and partner with you in oppression, Kingdom, and perseverance… When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. He laid his right hand on me, saying, 'Don't be afraid.'

> **1:9** I John, your brother and partner with you in oppression, Kingdom, and perseverance in Christ Jesus, was on the isle that is called Patmos because of God’s Word and the testimony of Jesus Christ.
> **1:10** I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a loud voice, like a trumpet
> **1:11** saying, “What you see, write in a book and send to the seven assemblies: to Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and to Laodicea.”
> **1:12** I turned to see the voice that spoke with me. Having turned, I saw seven golden lampstands.
> **1:13** And among the lampstands was one like a son of man, clothed with a robe reaching down to his feet, and with a golden sash around his chest.
> **1:14** His head and his hair were white as white wool, like snow. His eyes were like a flame of fire.
> **1:15** His feet were like burnished brass, as if it had been refined in a furnace. His voice was like the voice of many waters.
> **1:16** He had seven stars in his right hand. Out of his mouth proceeded a sharp two-edged sword. His face was like the sun shining at its brightest.
> **1:17** When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. He laid his right hand on me, saying, “Don’t be afraid. I am the first and the last,
> **1:18** and the Living one. I was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore. Amen. I have the keys of Death and of Hades.

**Claim.** The visionary introduces himself not by rank but by shared condition: 'I John, your brother and partner with you in oppression, Kingdom, and perseverance in Christ Jesus.' C13 and C25's endurance. The vision of the risen Christ is overwhelming — eyes like fire, a voice 'like the voice of many waters' — and John collapses: 'When I saw him, I fell at his feet like a dead man. He laid his right hand on me, saying, "Don't be afraid. I am the first and the last, and the Living one. I was dead, and behold, I am alive forevermore."' C24's awe, met with a hand and a reassurance.
